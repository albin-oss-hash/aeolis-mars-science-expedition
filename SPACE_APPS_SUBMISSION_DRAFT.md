# NASA Space Apps project page draft

## Project title

Aeolis: A Mars Science Expedition

## Short description

An educational Mars exploration game where players plan a science mission, explore Gale Crater, collect evidence, and send results to a science team on Earth.

## Project description

Aeolis puts the player in the role of a field geologist investigating an orbiter's hint of ancient water in Gale Crater. Players select a science objective and a compatible wavelength, instrument, and optical system, then explore Mars to collect basalt, clay-bearing rock, and dune samples. Returning to the lander lets players transmit the science packet and complete the story.

The prototype connects the mission lifecycle across launch and deployment, the spacecraft and orbital relay, surface operations, and science analysis. A mission journal introduces concept maturity stages, trade studies, point design, and verification in beginner-friendly language. The game also includes procedural Mars terrain, astronaut movement, a jetpack, dust-storm conditions, mission telemetry, and a relay-orbit schematic.

## NASA data and resources

This prototype was informed by the NASA and CSA resource descriptions supplied during development, including science-mission segments, mission design and concept maturity, GMAT-inspired trajectory visualization, telescope design, and CubeSat mission lifecycle ideas. It does not currently ingest a NASA or CSA dataset, use a NASA API, or connect to GMAT or NASA Mission Viz. Mission readouts and the orbital schematic are simulated for gameplay.

## How to run

Open `project.godot` with Godot 4.3 or newer. Controls: WASD to move, Shift to sprint, Space to jump, E for jetpack, F to collect samples, C to uplink near the lander, J to open the journal, F1 to toggle telemetry, and F2 to toggle the dust-storm drill.
